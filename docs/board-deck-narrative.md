# 12-Slide Board Narrative

1. **Engineering Intelligence Transformation Program** — From reactive engineering to supervised autonomous systems.
2. **The Scaling Problem** — Complexity, fragmented knowledge, MTTR, regression risk and architecture drift grow faster than headcount.
3. **The Strategic Opportunity** — Build a Private Engineering Intelligence Platform embedded in the SDLC.
4. **What This Is / Is Not** — Secure cognitive infrastructure, not a chatbot rollout or engineer replacement.
5. **Target Architecture** — Identity, private model access, RAG, policy, observability, Azure DevOps and AKS.
6. **Phase 1: Institutional Memory** — Citation-backed engineering knowledge, IDE integration and onboarding acceleration.
7. **Phase 2: Intelligent Guardrails** — PR Guardian, secure coding, IaC and architecture checks.
8. **Phase 3: Incident Intelligence** — Correlate telemetry with code/deployment history to reduce MTTR.
9. **Phase 4–5: Predictive + Self-Healing** — Change-risk scoring, drift detection, policy-approved remediation and verification.
10. **Financial Impact** — Reclaimed engineering capacity, fewer incidents, reduced escalation load and controlled AI spend.
11. **Governance and Safety** — RBAC, private networking, audit, human gates, runbook allow-lists and kill switches.
12. **Strategic Outcome** — Persistent knowledge, measurable risk, faster delivery and supervised autonomous operations.

## Board message
This is a platform modernization program, not an AI experiment. Investment should be released in phases against measurable engineering and risk-reduction outcomes.
