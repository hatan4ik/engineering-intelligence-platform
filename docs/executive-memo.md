# Executive Memo

## Subject
Engineering Intelligence Transformation Program: velocity, resilience and governed autonomy.

## Executive summary
Engineering organizations pay a scaling tax through fragmented knowledge, slow onboarding, repeat incidents, high MTTR, inconsistent architecture and senior-engineer bottlenecks. Adding headcount does not remove that structural friction.

The proposed program builds a **Private Engineering Intelligence Platform** that turns repositories, work items, architecture decisions, CI/CD history, telemetry and runbooks into a governed intelligence layer embedded in the SDLC.

## Decision requested
Approve a phased 18–24 month program with a 90-day foundation milestone and stage gates tied to measurable engineering outcomes.

## Strategic outcomes
- Faster knowledge retrieval and onboarding
- Faster, more consistent PR review
- Lower regression and security risk
- Faster incident triage and recovery
- Persistent institutional memory
- Predictive release-risk controls
- Guardrailed, auditable self-healing for known failure classes

## What this is
- Secure internal cognitive infrastructure
- Citation-backed RAG over enterprise engineering sources
- AI agents embedded in PR, deployment and incident workflows
- Policy-aware automation with bounded autonomy

## What this is not
- Public chatbot usage
- Unrestricted autonomous production mutation
- Replacement of engineering accountability

## Executive guardrails
Private networking, RBAC before retrieval, secret/PII controls, full auditability, human approval for high-risk actions, FinOps telemetry from day one, deterministic runbooks for mutation, and explicit kill switches.
